Yian Wang — data quality after the spatial update
30 October 2026. About 75–90 seconds; agree timing with Tabib and rehearse.

My work covers the data catalogue, provenance and quality checks. I checked six received event tables containing 2,846 satellite-derived event rows, and 56 weather points with complete time axes and no missing numeric values.

The original labels produce 2,822 positive cell-days. The new main pipeline excludes two probable static heat-source cells. I independently reproduced the resulting 2,684 positives across about 3.45 million cell-days, matching the published seasonal result counts. These populations should be named clearly whenever we report a rate.

The new published spatial-model CSVs favour LightGBM over the paired baseline. I checked their summary arithmetic, but did not retrain the model or reproduce scores from predictions. The static-source selection and inner-validation separation still need methodological review.

The weather reconstruction retains a failure at 35 grid centres outside the configured boundary. We also still need the actual forecast export and matching run provenance. These are explicit acceptance conditions, alongside agreeing UTC event dates and Perth weather days.
